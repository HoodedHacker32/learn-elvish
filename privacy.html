Privacy

Without an account, nothing you do in Pedo Edhellen leaves your device. With one, a copy of your progress is kept online so it follows you between devices, and that is all it is used for.

Last changed 11 November 2026.

Without an account

Your progress, settings and name are saved on your device only: in your browser’s storage for the web app, or in the app’s own folder for the Windows app. They are never sent anywhere.

There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The fonts are part of the app, so nothing is fetched from Google or any other font service. Reminders are made on your device; there is no push server.

Two things touch the internet without an account:

  • The website, the web app and the Windows download are served by GitHub, which keeps its own short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub privacy statement.
  • About once every half hour the app checks a small file on that site to see whether a newer version is out. The check sends nothing about you.

With an account

An account is optional. If you make one, this is kept online:

  • Your email address, to sign in and to send the emails an account needs (confirming it, and a link if you forget your password). It is never used for anything else: no newsletters, no marketing.
  • Your password, which is stored only as a one-way hash by our account provider. Nobody, including us, can read it.
  • A copy of your progress: lessons done, what you have learned and when to review it, practice days and settings.
  • A name and a picture, if you add them. The picture is cropped to a 96 × 96 square on your device before it is sent, so the original never leaves it. It can be seen by anyone who has its web address, which contains only a random account number.
  • The times you signed up, last signed in and last synced.

We use this only to run your account and keep your progress in step across your devices. In UK and EU data protection terms, the reason (lawful basis) is that it is needed to provide the service you asked for.

The data is stored by Supabase, which runs the accounts for us, in a data centre in London, in the UK. Supabase handles it only on our behalf, under its data processing terms. Nobody else gets it, and it is never sold or shared.

Seeing, changing and deleting it

  • See it: everything kept online is shown in the app, under your profile and on the path.
  • Change it: your name, picture and password can be changed in your profile at any time.
  • Delete it: “Delete my account” in your profile deletes your account, your picture and the online copy of your progress at once. Your progress on the device stays, and you can keep learning without an account.

Accounts that are deleted are gone from the live database straight away. Our provider may hold them in its own short-lived backups for a few days more.

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. The course itself needs no account and is suitable for any age.

Who runs it

Pedo Edhellen is a free, unofficial fan project, run by its maintainer under the name HoodedHacker. For anything about your data, open an issue on GitHub without putting any personal details in it, and we will arrange a private way to reply.

If you are in the UK and unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office; in the EU, to your country’s data protection authority.
