Get the app

The same course, in your browser or on Windows. If you are not sure, start in the browser: there is nothing to install.

  1. annon“gate”, as in annon edhellen, “Elvish gate”. The Fellowship of the Ring, Book II, ch. 4.

In your browser

Opens straight away on a computer, tablet or phone, and keeps working offline once it has loaded. To keep it like an app, choose “Install app” in your browser’s menu, or “Add to Home Screen” on a phone.

Open the web app {{icon:arrow}}

Your profile and progress are saved in that browser. The daily reminder can only arrive while the app is open or installed.

For Windows

The full app in its own window, with one gentle daily reminder as a Windows notification.

{{icon:down}} Install for Windows {{setupSize}}

Installs for you alone (no administrator needed), with a Start menu shortcut, and starts faster. Or take the portable version {{size}}, which runs without installing, from anywhere, even a USB stick. Both keep your progress in the same place, so you can switch.

The app is not signed yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time you open it. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Closing the window keeps it in the system tray so the reminder can arrive; quit it from the tray icon.
