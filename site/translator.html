Five ratings, from certain to refused

Every result carries one of these, in the translator’s own words:

  1. Tolkien’s exact wordsA line he wrote, quoted with its source.

  3. Built from Tolkien’s wordsEvery word his, joined with grammar he showed. Composed, not quoted.
  4. Partly reconstructedReadable, with each piece that is not his listed.
