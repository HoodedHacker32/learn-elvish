# Research notes

Only peer-reviewed papers or primary sources were used. Blog posts from apps or course sellers were skipped.

## How people learn and keep vocabulary

- **Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan & Willingham (2013).** *Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning
  Techniques.* Psychological Science in the Public Interest 14(1). Ten techniques rated: practice testing and
  distributed practice "high"; interleaving, elaborative interrogation and self-explanation "moderate";
  summarising, highlighting and rereading "low". Testing works best with corrective feedback.
  Summary: https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/which-study-strategies-make-the-grade.html
- **Cepeda, Vul, Rohrer, Wixted & Pashler (2008).** *Spacing effects in learning: a temporal ridgeline of optimal
  retention.* Psychological Science 19(11). 1,350+ learners; the best gap rises with the retention interval
  (about 20–40% of a one-week delay, 5–10% of a one-year delay). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19076480/
- **Kim & Webb (2022).** Meta-analysis of spacing in L2 vocabulary (48 experiments, N = 3,411): spaced learners
  recalled more words.
- **Brunmair & Richter (2019).** *Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved learning.* Psychological
  Bulletin. Interleaving helps most when the items are easy to confuse.
- **Webb (2005).** *Receptive and productive vocabulary learning.* Studies in Second Language Acquisition. Productive
  tasks build more of a word's knowledge. https://cambridge.org/core/journals/studies-in-second-language-acquisition/article/receptive-and-productive-vocabulary-learning-the-effects-of-reading-and-writing-on-word-knowledge/DDF362AE7B13D1949B1CD591DA2F3414
- **Barcroft (2007, 2015).** Retrieval opportunities and output during L2 vocabulary learning.
- **Settles & Meeder (2016).** *A Trainable Spaced Repetition Model for Language Learning.* ACL. Half-life
  regression. https://aclanthology.org/P16-1174/
- **FSRS** (Open Spaced Repetition; Ye et al., KDD and IEEE TKDE). A modern open scheduler benchmarked on large
  review logs; a candidate replacement for the fixed intervals once there is enough review history.
  https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition

## Motivation, streaks and harm

- **Silverman & Barasch (2023).** *On or Off Track: How (Broken) Streaks Affect Consumer Decisions.* Journal of
  Consumer Research. Intact streaks raise later engagement; broken ones lower it, more when self-blamed, less when
  repairable; the authors advise apps not to dwell on broken streaks.
  https://udspace.udel.edu/items/42ce576b-8e1f-429a-8541-e29e48dbcfbb/full
- **Kuo, Zhao & Scott.** Exit dark patterns in gamified educational apps (IDC). "Goal drift" from mastery to
  metric maintenance; "gamified obligation" (guilt about leaving).
  https://deceptive.design/articles/the-evil-bird-and-the-right-to-disconnect-children-s-vulnerability-to-exit-dark-patterns-in-gamified-educational-apps
- **Gray et al. (CHI 2020).** *UI Dark Patterns and Where to Find Them.* 95% of 240 popular apps contained at least one.
- **Monge Roffarello et al. (CHI 2023).** Attention-capture damaging patterns, including fake social notifications.
  https://elite.polito.it/news/2023/05/04/chi-attention-capture
- **Self-Determination Theory in language apps.** Evidence is mostly small and self-reported (e.g. Jeon 2022,
  Education and Information Technologies), but competence support appears consistently; treated as a design
  guide, not a proven effect. https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/2022_Jeon_ExploringaSelf-Directed.pdf
