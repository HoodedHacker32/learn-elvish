Credits

The Elvish languages are J.R.R. Tolkien’s. Everything else here stands on the work of people who studied them, drew their letters and built free tools.

The words

  • J.R.R. Tolkien invented Sindarin, Quenya and the Tengwar. The lines quoted in the course are his, from The Lord of the Rings, The Silmarillion and his published papers. Pedo Edhellen is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Tolkien Estate.
  • Eldamo, the Elvish lexicon by Paul Strack (CC BY 4.0), is the source for every word, its meaning, its stage of Tolkien’s writing and whether it is his or made by fans.

The letters and fonts

  • Alcarin Tengwar by Toshi Omagari (SIL Open Font License) sets all the Tengwar, and its outlines are the model for the handwriting strokes.
  • The stroke order for writing each letter follows Måns Björkman’s Amanyë Tenceli.
  • The Annatar keyboard layout is Dan Smith’s encoding, as documented by Johan Winge for Tengwar Annatar.
  • Tengwar numerals are written as in Tolkien’s own samples and the Tecendil handbook.
  • Figtree by Erik Kennedy and Marcellus by Astigmatic (both SIL Open Font License) set the rest of the text.

The code

  • Handwriting is recognised with the $P point-cloud recogniser by Radu-Daniel Vatavu, Lisa Anthony and Jacob O. Wobbrock (2012).
  • Built with React, Vite and Electron; accounts run on Supabase. All are open source.
  • The source code is public.

How AI was used

Pedo Edhellen was made by one person working with AI models by Anthropic: Claude Opus 5.5, and Claude Sonnet 5.5 for testing passes. They wrote most of the code and drafted much of the teaching text, under the maintainer’s direction. Everything was tested by a human, on real devices.

AI is good at sounding sure of Elvish it has made up, so the course does not trust it: every Elvish word is checked against Eldamo and labelled with where it comes from, and anything that is not Tolkien’s own is marked as fan-made on every screen.

The app itself uses no AI: nothing you type or write is sent to an AI service, and the handwriting is checked on your own device.
